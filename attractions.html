Explore Local Attractions

Downtown Raleigh offers a variety of attractions for visitors of all ages.

Marbles Kids Museum

An interactive museum with hands-on exhibits and activities for children and families.

  • Neighborhood: Moore Square
  • Address: 201 East Hargett Street, Raleigh, NC

Before your visit, check the official Marbles Kids Museum website for current hours, tickets, and visitor information.

North Carolina State Capitol

A historic landmark where visitors can learn about North Carolina's history and government.

  • Address: 1 East Edenton Street, Raleigh, NC
  • Parking: Metered spaces around Capitol Square or the Museum/Government Center lot at 100 East Jones Street.

Before your visit, check the official North Carolina State Capitol website for tour information, opening hours, and accessibility details.

Shopping & Local Gems

Leave a little room in your day for browsing. Discover gifts, creative finds, and something special to remember your Raleigh visit.

DECO Raleigh

Browse stationery, jewelry, home accessories, and North Carolina-made goods. DECO supports local artists and makers, making it a fun stop for a thoughtful gift or a little treat for yourself.

  • Good for: Gifts, stationery, and local finds
  • Address: 207 South Salisbury Street, Raleigh, NC

Explore DECO Raleigh and check store details

Father & Son Antiques

Add a vintage treasure hunt to your downtown day. Browse clothing, furniture, accessories, and vinyl records for a find with a little history and personality.

  • Good for: Vintage clothing, records, and home finds
  • Neighborhood: Warehouse District
  • Address: 302 South West Street, Raleigh, NC
